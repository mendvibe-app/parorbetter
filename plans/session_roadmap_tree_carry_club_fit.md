# Tree Carry & Club-Fit — Session Roadmap

Compiled from a full playtest pass on Apex Carry + Shot Clearance
Feedback, plus everything it surfaced about club selection and dispersion.
Recommended build order below, with rationale for the ordering.

## Shipped

- **Tree Apex Carry** — club loft, per-tree canopy height, height-aware flight collision.
- **Shot Clearance Feedback** — aim cone red/blocked or green/clear from clean-strike height prediction.
- **Apex / Canopy Rebalance** — raised short-iron/wedge loft_mul; compressed mid canopies; tall stays hard wall.
- **Aim point persistence** — Change Club keeps aim bearing; refits distance for new club.
- **Club-Fit Consequences** — force (from true_pct) shortens distance + widens aim circle when out of pocket.
- **Tree Punch-Out** — Trees-only Punch toggle: low apex, more roll, ducks under canopy band.
- **Shot Shape from Path** — full-swing `max_lateral` blends with hole shape; contact gates authority.
